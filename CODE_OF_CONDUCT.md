# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

CollabSpace is intended to be a welcoming, respectful, and collaborative space for people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives. We want participation to be harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, disability, appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, nationality, religion (or lack thereof), gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, education, or level of experience.

We expect everyone in the community to act professionally, communicate respectfully, and help maintain an environment where people can contribute and learn.

## Expected behaviour

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Showing empathy and respect toward other people.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focusing criticism on ideas and code rather than attacking people.
- Respecting different viewpoints and experience levels.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Respecting people's privacy and boundaries.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include:

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or demeaning language and conduct.
- Sexualized language or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or repeated bad-faith arguments.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably make the community unsafe or hostile.

## Responsibilities of maintainers

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards in this Code of Conduct. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this policy, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation where appropriate.

Maintainers should apply these standards fairly and proportionately.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to:

**[INSERT MAINTAINER CONTACT OR PRIVATE REPORTING CHANNEL BEFORE PUBLICATION]**

Please include, if possible, what happened, where it happened, when it happened, and any relevant links or evidence. Do not post sensitive details publicly when a private report is more appropriate.

Maintainers will review reports as promptly as reasonably possible and handle them discreetly to the extent possible. Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed in every circumstance. Do not retaliate against someone for making a good-faith report.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within CollabSpace project spaces and when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces. It also applies when project participation affects the safety or well-being of community members.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may respond to violations with one or more of the following measures, depending on severity and context:

1. A private warning and request to stop the behaviour.
2. A temporary restriction from relevant project spaces or interactions.
3. Temporary or permanent removal from the community or project spaces.

Maintainers will aim to respond proportionately and consistently. A person responsible for enforcement should not handle a report where they have a conflict of interest; where possible, another maintainer should review it.
